# Northwind Robotics Employee Handbook

## Working hours

Core working hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time, Monday through Friday. Outside core hours employees may organise their schedule freely, as long as they work 40 hours per week on average over a month.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote arrangements require written approval from the department head and are reviewed every six months. The company reimburses up to 50 EUR per month for home internet.

## Vacation

Every employee receives 28 calendar days of paid vacation per year. Up to 7 unused days can be carried over to the next year; carried-over days expire on March 31. Vacation requests longer than 10 days must be submitted at least one month in advance.

## Sick leave

Employees must notify their manager before 10:00 on the first day of illness. A doctor's certificate is required from the third consecutive day of absence.

## Equipment

New employees choose between a 14-inch or 16-inch laptop. Laptops are replaced every three years. Lost or damaged equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours.

## Learning budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,200 EUR for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not carry over. Conference trips abroad additionally require manager approval.
